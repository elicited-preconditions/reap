Parts of baselines/ploi/ are ported or adapted from github.com/tomsilver/ploi. They are covered by the MIT licence of
that repository, reproduced verbatim below from its LICENSE.md:

  baselines/ploi/ploi/model.py     the Encode-Process-Decode graph network and its MLP helper, ported from gnn/gnn.py
                          (EncodeProcessDecode, GraphModel, MLP)
  baselines/ploi/ploi/labels.py    the single-plan labelling, single_plan_touched_objects, ported from
                          guidance/gnn_guidance.py (_collect_training_data)
  baselines/ploi/ploi/features.py  the graph encoding of a problem (type one-hot, unary initial-state and goal features,
                          binary features in both directions, edges only where a predicate holds), adapted from
                          guidance/gnn_guidance.py (_state_to_graph)

The other files of baselines/ploi/ are not derived from tomsilver/ploi; they fall under this repository's licences
(LICENSE and LICENSES/ at the repository root).
